Share of world AI compute by region over time

Four views of the same regional estimate, from composition to absolute growth. Data pulled 20 August 2026.

Scope: EOY 2023 includes shipments from 2022–2023. Chips shipped in 2021 and earlier are outside the model for every region; the model does not retire chips.

Final candidate: 1e (consolidated)

Circle area is proportional to consolidated regional share of deployed FP8 GB300-equivalent compute. Each year-end includes direct and legal deployment flows through Q3; smuggling counts full calendar years.

Other combines East Asia ex-China (1.8%), India (1.5%), Australia & NZ (0.7%), the prior Other category (0.7%), and Latin America (0.6%). Unrounded 2026E shares sum to 5.2%; the displayed component shares sum to approximately 5.3% because of rounding.

Source: TrendForce production aggregates, Epoch deployment data; our estimates.

1f. Narrow bars with connectors (consolidated)

Bar height uses one common scale for consolidated regional share of deployed FP8 GB300-equivalent compute; bars are vertically centered within each row. Each year-end includes direct and legal deployment flows through Q3; smuggling counts full calendar years.

Every bar is labeled; faint lines connect adjacent bar tops and bottoms. Shares below 2.5% use a two-unit minimum display height for visibility. Other uses the same consolidated definition as the final candidate above.

Source: TrendForce production aggregates, Epoch deployment data; our estimates.
