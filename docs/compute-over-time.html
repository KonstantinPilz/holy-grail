Share of world AI compute by region over time

Four views of the same regional estimate, from composition to absolute growth. Data pulled 20 August 2026.

Scope: EOY 2023 includes shipments from 2022–2023. Chips shipped in 2021 and earlier are outside the model for every region; the model does not retire chips.

Source: TrendForce production aggregates, Epoch deployment data; our estimates.
